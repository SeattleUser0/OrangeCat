# MACRO2: the rest of Plaits as a module (a candidate study)

**Version:** 0.1 (November 2, 2026; branch macro2-candidate, cut from main d61340e = v0.15.0 plus three workflow commits; host work only, nothing flashed)
**Reads:** docs/orangecat/BUILD.md phase 7 (how MACRO was ported), ADDING_AN_ENGINE.md (the hot-set rule, the batch), ENGINE_CANDIDATES.md (the gate), ENGINE_LOADING_PLAN.md ("What runs from where, measured"), MODULE_FORMAT_R3.md, FLASH_MAP_R3.md, RELEASE3_PLAN.md, SIZE_AUDIT.md, GUI_SLIM.md
**Two reports:** part 1 is MACRO2 (sections 1 to 8); part 2 is the "voice mode" profile of the current linux-hw build set (section 9).

## 0. The answer in short

- **The port is done and exact.** The ten models MACRO lacks (wavetable, speech, and Plaits 1.2's second bank: six-op FM, wave terrain, string machine, chiptune, phase distortion, VA with filter) are in C under firmware/src/plaits/, ported the way MACRO was (BUILD.md phase 7), and tests/plaits_parity now compares all 24 models: **24 of 24 models, 96 of 96 scenarios, difference 0**, with and without the firmware's denormal flushes (section 2).
- **As one module it does not fit anywhere.** The full MACRO2 image is **107,960 B** (29,952 B of code, 76,688 B of tables, 764 B of metadata): 27 sectors against today's 12 (49,152 B) and Release 3's 18 (73,728 B). The tables are the problem, not the code: the 192 waves are 50,688 B, the three DX7 banks 12,288 B, the five speech word banks 11,011 B (section 4).
- **The second bank alone fits today.** Without wavetable and speech (and with wave terrain's three wavetable terrains out, so no wave data is needed) the module is **35,228 B, 9 sectors**: it fits today's 12-sector area alone (not beside SHAPES or the Prophet), and Release 3's 18 beside SHAPES (15) or beside the Prophet (17), never beside both. That is the shippable candidate: six-op FM with its three DX7 banks (96 patches), wave terrain, string machine, chiptune, phase distortion and VA-VCF (section 5).
- **Wavetable and speech are a second question.** Together they are 77,160 B (19 sectors: more than Release 3's whole area). Speech alone is about 20.5 KB (6 sectors). The wavetable at full size is about 53.4 KB (14 sectors, Release 3 alone only); with two of its three banks about 36.5 KB (9 sectors). Storing the waves with fewer bits is not an option: the engine differentiates them, and 8-bit waves give 14.5 dB of signal to noise (section 6).
- **RAM is no problem:** 4,720 B a track (MACRO takes 11,320), four voices for the small models, one for six-op, string machine and speech. Speech needed one real change to get there (its word bank decodes on the fly: 520 B instead of 13.5 KB), exact to the sample (section 3).
- **Cost on the unit is unmeasured** (host only here). The host's render costs per voice against MACRO's VA and the gate's static loop costs are in section 7 with the voice units they suggest; six-op FM (two internal voices, six operators each: loop cost 1,837 against the Prophet's 1,075) and the string machine (821) are the heavy ones. The unit session's commands are at the end of section 7.
- **Voice mode** (section 9): of the current linux-hw image (546,224 B), the screen is 172 KB, the tracker and sequencer 57 KB of flash and 80 KB of RAM, the editor link 34 KB, the effects 32 KB, projects and storage 32 KB, the card 27 KB of flash and 43 KB of RAM, the recorder 12 KB of flash and 51 KB of RAM. The engines (MELODEE_* switches, SHAPES already a module), DUB TAPE, USB audio, OTA and the new interface's steps are the only parts a switch takes out today; the tracker, arranger, performance mode, recorder, card and the rest of the effects are tangled into the main loop, the audio interrupt and the screen and come out only with work. And image bytes are not module bytes: what leaves the image makes room for built-in engines (or for FLASH_MAP_R3's option A), not for larger modules.

## 2. The port (tests/plaits_parity: 24 of 24 models exact)

## 3. The engine: eng_macro2.c and the module

**One engine, MACRO's glue.** firmware/src/eng_macro2.c is eng_macro.c with the other model list: the same pages (MODEL: MODL HARM TIMB MRPH; SHAPE: AUX LPG DECY TMOD), the same voice handling (four voices a track in POLY, the ADSR as the VCA, the LPG knob, the DECAY envelope on TIMBRE, the module's attenuverter curve), the same split of each voice's block into a CPU0 control part and a kernel from a descriptor (ADDING_AN_ENGINE.md 5a; the kernel pushed under AP_MACRO, since a new profile id would move FW_API_VERSION). The models that draw Plaits' shared generator (CHIP's arpeggiator, SPEECH's LPC noise, 6OP's sample-and-hold LFO) render at once on CPU0 in voice order, as MACRO's noise models do. The output levels and the "already enveloped" flags are voice.cc's (six-op is enveloped; speech says so per render: a replayed word is, a held vowel is not, and the LPG follows it).

**Models and voices.** VA-VCF, PD, TERRAN, CHIP, WAVTBL: four voices a track (their states are 24 to 448 B). 6OP, STRMCH, SPEECH: one voice a track (6OP keeps the module's own two internal voices, so a new note still overlaps the last one's release; STRMCH's ensemble line is 4 KB; SPEECH is one voice on the module too). 6OP A, B and C are three models, one a DX7 bank, as Plaits registers the engine three times; HARM picks the patch within the bank (32).

**State a track** (mc2_part_t, the header's state_size): **4,720 B** on the device (4,728 on the 64-bit host): the model byte, four voice heads (the LPG filter, two limiters, the note, the accent, the decay envelope: 44 B each) and the union of the models: the small ones four times (VA-VCF 140 B, PD 80, TERRAN 24, CHIP 448, WAVTBL 52: 1,792 B for four CHIPs), or 6OP 2,656 B, or STRMCH 4,540 B (the largest: the union's size), or SPEECH 520 B. Under the loader's 16 KB limit and MACRO's 12 KB a part with room to spare; four tracks take 18.9 KB of the arena. Nothing is allocated: every scratch buffer (the terrain's and PD's 384 B, six-op's 384 B, speech's 192 B) is on the kernel's stack; the deepest render stack is speech's, about 770 B on the target, under CPU1's 8 KB.

**The module** (tools/module/macro2_mod.c; the host unit tests/module_host_macro2_tu.c) is built the way wheel_mod.c builds WHEEL: the engine's source included as it is, the firmware's services through fw_api_t (trk, resource_get, tuning_log, the pack's ready flag, the batch queue, memcpy and memset), the header carrying ENGI_MACRO2, the state size, the pack hash (MACRO2 reads pl_lut_sine and the two pitch-ratio tables from the asset pack, so it is silent without the pack, as MACRO) and the hot set. Two things a module cannot have had to go: Plaits' shared random word (pl_dsp.h's `pl_rng_state`, 4 B of .data) is the firmware's `rng_cell` through the table (the word fw_api_t keeps for modules, SHAPES' pb_rng_state; MACRO2's drawing models render on CPU0 in voice order, so sharing it with SHAPES keeps every draw deterministic), and the engine's own hot-set pointer array (16 B of .data, a built-in engine's) is left out of the module (a module reads its copies through fw_api_t.hot). The build's checks pass: no undefined symbol (the pi32v2 clang turns a struct copy into a memcpy call, which the speech decoder avoids by copying frames field by field), **.data 0 B, .bss 0 B**, the header's fields, the relocatable package (format 2) for its one start.

**The engine number.** 32, proposed, in an OC_CANDIDATES build only (core.h ENGI_MACRO2; NENGINES 33 there, 20 shipping), as SEM, HADES and op1r's numbers are: the first free number past op1r's reserved 24..31. One consequence: 0.14.1's 32-bit engine mask (engines.c eng_hs.refused) is 64 bits wide now. The measurement build's table, order, retirement rule, module slot rule and geometry row (12 sectors at start 0, so the shrunk variant of section 5 can register) carry it; the shipping build is unchanged. As the other candidates past 15, it holds no user preset and no favorite until upreset.c and favorites.c learn larger numbers.

**Release 3's module format** (MODULE_FORMAT_R3.md): MACRO2 brings no fw_pages_t descriptor (its eight controls are the firmware's EDIT 1 and EDIT 2 for any engine, as WHEEL and the Caravel candidates), so fw_pages version 3 changes nothing in it beyond the one rebuild every module gets when the identifier moves; a HOME picture and four knob declarations (fw_ui_t, fw_kdef_t) could be added in a later module version without a second identifier move, the format's point. The kernel's push under AP_MACRO stays as long as the profile ids stay. What Release 3 does change is where it could live: FLASH_MAP_R3.md's 18 sectors with a start at every sector (section 5). Not built for it: the branch is cut from main, with the 12-sector loader and its three starts.

**Hot set, per the 0.13.7 rule** (ENG_HOT_LIST in eng_macro2.c; what each model reads inside its sample loop, by reading the loops as tools/pack_hot.py does):

Declared (MACRO2_HOT=1, the default): **pl_lut_sine, pl_lut_pitch_ratio_high, pl_lut_pitch_ratio_low (the pack's; MACRO declares the same three) and pl_lut_lpc_excitation_pulse (the module's): 5,252 B of arena.** The pitch tables are read once a block by every model, but they are tiny and MACRO's set has them (one copy serves the engine, and the loader accepts the pack as a source). What is not declared: the 50,688 B of waves. They cannot be (16 KB a table, and the arena), so WAVTBL and the top of TERRAN read them from the module's flash through the D-cache. What that costs is not known without the unit: the reads are sequential within a wave (eight waves of 264 B at a time, the phase advancing slowly; the waves that are read together sit 264 B, 2,112 B and 16,896 B apart, which is 264, 2,112 and 512 modulo 4 KiB: no two of them share the one direct-mapped way's lines), so a miss costs a line fill (about 280 ns) once a line, not once a read, and most reads should hit. The gate's advice holds (ENGINE_CANDIDATES.md, the Prophet): a module declares a hot set only where measured; MACRO2_HOT=0 builds the A/B (code 29,756 against 29,952 B: the four pointer chains cost 196 B). The measured answer belongs to the unit session (section 7).

**Tests (host, all green):** tests/plaits_parity (above); tests/module_host_cand_test.c built for MACRO2 (the suite's module_host_candidates step): the header passes the registration check, the slot is retired without the module and taken with it, every preset renders through the whole voice path with its line in tests/golden_modules.txt (20 new lines, module/MACRO2/...); tests/macro2_test.c (new; the suite's step after it): every model and preset sounds and frees its voice, a model change under a held note through all ten, a four-note chord on VA-VCF plays four voices and one on each large model, four tracks of the heaviest models at once, and the host cost table of section 7. The compile of every header alone with -Wall -Wextra is clean, and with the pi32v2 clang's flags (-Os -Werror=double-promotion -fno-builtin) too.

## 6. What could shrink, and what it does to the sound

- **The waves at fewer bits: no.** Plaits stores the waves integrated (the engine differentiates them after a Hermite interpolation, wavetable_engine.cc), so quantizing the stored table amplifies the error on the way out. Measured on the 192 waves (tools: the generator's data, the engine's difference): 8-bit storage gives **14.5 dB** of signal to noise over all waves (6.6 dB on the worst wave); 10-bit 26.3 dB (17.1 worst); 12-bit 38.3 dB (27.4 worst). None is the module's sound. Storing the waveform itself at 8 bits with a per-wave scale would be 47 dB, but that is another engine (no integrated Hermite, another reader), not a port, and still 25 KB.
- **Fewer wave banks: the honest cut.** Each bank is 64 waves, 16,896 B. Bank 3 is the "shuffled" bank (every wave of the first three at i x 101 mod 192) and costs nothing. With two banks the knob's bank axis spans two and the fold at the top (z0, z1 >= 4 fold to 7 - z) repeats them; what is lost is the 64 waves of the bank left out (bank 2, "the harmonic / additive bank", is the natural one to drop, keeping the analog-style bank 0 and the digital bank 1). The wave terrain's terrains 5..7 use the three banks too (2, 1, 0 in that order): with bank 2 out the terrain's top eighth of the knob goes, or terrain 5 repeats bank 1.
- **DX7 banks.** 4,096 B a bank, 32 patches each: bank 0 is basses and leads (SOLID BASS, Mooger Low, LeaderTape, CS 80, SPIRAL, FAIRLIGHT, the PPG and Vocoder patches), bank 1 keys, mallets, bells and drums (E.PIANO 1, FENDER 1, Mark III, CLAVINET, STEINWAY, SITAR, KOTO, MARIMBA, VIBE 1, TUB BELLS, GONG 2, the drums), bank 2 organs, pads, strings and brass (Hammond 1, PIPES, M1 PADS, Planets, VANGELIS 1, STRINGS C, FULL STRIN, BRASS 1, BR TRUMPET). One bank saves 8,192 B and loses the other 64 patches outright; which bank is a taste question (a module build could carry any one, or two, by a switch: MACRO2_SET is the pattern). The 960 B algorithm table in each track's state is the same for every track (a pure function of the const opcodes) and could be a 960 B table in flash instead: 960 B of arena a track, one wave's worth of module.
- **Speech word banks.** 1,233 to 4,802 B a bank; bank 4 alone is 44 % of the words' bytes (22 words, the longest frames). Dropping banks drops words: the knob's LPC half spans the banks that are there (the quantizer's step count is the bank count), so nothing else moves. The phoneme bank (the vowels and consonants, 210 B) and the naive and SAM synths (no large tables) stay whatever is dropped.
- **Code.** Six-op's eight template instantiations are already one renderer; speech's three synths cannot share; the glue is MACRO's. A build without a model saves its code (section 4) and its state's share of the union; MACRO2_SET 1 and 2 are the two cuts that make sense (no wave data, or only the two models that need it).

**The point about where freed bytes go.** The image lives in the app area (0x05000..0x90FFF, 573,440 B, encrypted, written only by the update loader); the module area (0xC8000.., 12 sectors; 18 in Release 3) is elsewhere, plaintext, written by the installer. Taking a feature out of the image frees image bytes: room for a **built-in** engine (MACRO2's full 108 KB would fit in the image if the tracker, the card, the recorder and DRUM-X left it: 56.7 + 27.5 + 12.1 + 8.3 = 104.6 KB, with 27 KB free today), or for FLASH_MAP_R3.md's option A (the app area's tail given to a second module region: 4 sectors, after a loader change). It does not make a module fit the module area. So "every feature removable as a module so bigger engines fit" has two halves: features as modules (the effects plan's path: a feature in the module area takes module sectors, which is the scarce thing; a feature compiled out by a switch takes none), and engines in the freed image (the cheap path, with the hot-set rule already built for built-in engines). The RAM side is simpler: the tracker, the recorder and the card hold 175 KB of RAM between them; freed, it is arena (the pool's tail), which is what larger engine states and more voices need (MACRO2's 6OP with four voices a track would be 10.6 KB a track; a four-voice STRMCH 18 KB).

**Which features are already separable (a switch exists and the build is green with it off):** the engines (MELODEE_MACRO, MELODEE_DRUMX, and the retired ones; SHAPES is out already), DUB TAPE (MELODEE_DUB), USB audio (MELODEE_USB_AUDIO), TRS MIDI (MELODEE_UART), the updater (MELODEE_OTA), all storage (MELODEE_FLASH), the new interface's steps and the ui-fix items (OC_HOME_PIC, OC_EDIT_SHEETS, OC_SHIFT_MAP, OC_STEP_PAGES, OC_LANES, OC_MIXER, OC_CLIP, OC_ONE_AUTH, OC_SLIDE, OC_REC_CLEAR, OC_PROJECT_PAGE, OC_SLIDE_TIME, OC_START_NUDGE), the modules themselves (OC_MODULES), and the diagnostics (OC_CORE_STATS, OC_HW_TEST, OC_SFC_PROBE). **Tangled, no switch:** the tracker and sequencer, the arranger, the recorder, the card (USB host, FatFs, files, streams, player), the performance layer, the slicer and the bus core, projects and the record store, the editor's command set, and the screen's pages for all of them. Each of those needs the same work the engines got in phase 3: a switch, its pages' rows compiled out, its editor commands behind it, its RAM arrays behind it, and the host suite green both ways, before it can become a module or a build variant. The order that pays: the tracker with projects and the pattern pool (57 KB of flash, 125 KB of RAM, and seq.c out of the audio path), then the card with the recorder (40 KB of flash, 94 KB of RAM), then the performance layer and the arranger (15 KB).

**Not measured here:** what a voice-mode image would actually be after those removals (the shared code the attribution cannot split, the strings, the pages); the unit's CPU with the sequencer gone (seq_step and the pattern poll leave the audio interrupt); the installer and editor page changes a build variant would need (the set manifests already carry per-build module lists).
